About controls & connection

Only lights have direct controls. Other devices are status-only. Flows and Moods always ask for confirmation; Flows can affect alarms or other equipment.

Requests use Copilot's normal tool permissions and your existing Homey sign-in. No API keys or device snapshots are stored on disk by this extension.

If Homey reports an authentication error, open Customize > Installed > homey > Sign in, then refresh. No action is retried automatically.
